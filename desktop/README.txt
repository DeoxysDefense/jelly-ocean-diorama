Jelly Ocean Diorama 1.0 - desktop version
=========================================

1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent (for example Documents\Jelly Ocean).
   The shortcut points at this folder, so do not delete or move it afterwards.
2. Double-click "Create Desktop Shortcut.cmd" once.
3. Open "Jelly Ocean" from your desktop or the Start menu.

It opens in its own window (Microsoft Edge app mode). F11 = full screen.
You can also simply double-click index.html to open it in your browser.

Everything runs offline; nothing is downloaded or sent anywhere.
Your world, settings and coconut timers are saved locally in the browser storage.

To remove: delete the "Jelly Ocean" shortcuts and this folder.
